6. **`seldon_commit_governed` commits and never pushes; the only push is the dispatcher's `push_if_ahead` at a merged finish (AD-034, AD-034-R12).** This thread committed squiddy c09884157ba1 and seldon 428a4c897fc0 through it. squiddy's dispatcher is live, so its commit leaves at that repository's next merged finish. seldon's open queue held nothing in flight at 2026-11-03 13:36 UTC (FUTURE items, one blocked task, stale defects), so 428a4c89, and this addendum, stay on the local main until some unrelated seldon task merges. A Desktop commit therefore reaches its remote on a schedule set by whatever else happens to run, and in an idle repository not at all. The operator's statement, 2026-11-03: he will not run anything manually, a push included, and treats a step that needs him to push as not factory thinking. The Desktop thread has no shell on the operator's machine to push from: its one attempt to open one (a folder-access request) was refused by the session's safety classifier.
